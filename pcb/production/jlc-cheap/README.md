# WSNES: cheap prototype assembly variant

Same board and same gerbers as `../jlc/`. Only the split between JLC assembly and hand assembly changes, which makes the JLC bill smaller:

- **JLC leaves off four parts:**
  - U1 (CY62148ELL-55SXI): only 5 were in stock at LCSC on 2026-11-18, so a JLC order could stall on it. It is sourced separately and hand-soldered (SOIC-32, 1.27 mm pitch).
  - SW1, SW2 and U23: optional.
- **The green and blue LEDs (D3, D7) become the red JLC basic LED**, KT-0603R `C2286`. They use the same 0603 pads and 1 kΩ resistors, so they run at about 3 mA on 5 V and 1.3 mA on 3.3 V. All three LEDs are then red.
- **17 extended part types** remain, about 51 USD in feeder fees at 3 USD per type.

**Hardware v0.4 status (2026-11-18):** the v0.4 board is placed but not yet routed, and there are no v0.4 gerbers yet. The CSVs in this folder already match the v0.4 placement (parts on both sides). `../jlc/WSNES_Proto_v0.3_gerbers.zip` belongs to the v0.3 board and must not be combined with these CSVs.

Generated by `../../tools/export_cheap.py` (from `pcb/`: `python3 tools/export_cheap.py .`, after `tools/export_bom.py`). Prices and stock were checked on 2026-11-18.

## Ordering at JLC

1. Gerbers, with these settings:
   - 4 layers, 1.2 mm, ENIG
   - gold fingers with 45° bevel
   - PCB quantity 5 (JLC's minimum)
2. **PCB Assembly**:
   - **both sides** (Standard PCBA; Economic PCBA is top-side only)
   - **PCBA quantity 2.** Not all 5 boards need assembly: JLC assembles 2 and ships the other 3 bare.
3. Upload `WSNES_BOM_JLC_cheap.csv` and `WSNES_CPL_JLC_cheap.csv`. On the parts page, every line should be matched and in stock.
4. Check rotations in the placement preview, on both sides. SOT-23, SOT-23-5 and SOT-23-6 parts often need 180°, and bottom-side parts are shown mirrored.
5. The last page shows the price, without placing an order.

The quote page shows what each PCB option costs. ENIG plus gold fingers is the expensive part. HASL edge fingers work for a bench prototype but wear faster in the slot.

## Notes

- **ESP variant:** any ESP32-S3-WROOM-1 (N4, N8, N16, with or without PSRAM) fits the footprint, because the board doesn't use GPIO35–37. Only the castellated edge pads need solder; the centre GND pad is optional.
- **Flashing without SW1/SW2:** with USB-C plugged in, esptool resets the S3 into download mode over native USB (USB-Serial/JTAG). If firmware ever takes over GPIO19/20 and that stops working, bridging the SW1 pads (GPIO0 to GND) while plugging in, or fitting the button, restores it.
- **U16 (CY62128ELL-45SXI)** is rated 4.5–5.5 V. It sits on VSAVE, one BAT54C drop (≈ 0.35 V) below +5V, so it is only inside its rating while the console delivers about 4.9 V or more. Data retention from the CR2032 is fine (from 2.0 V). When the AS6C1008-55SIN (2.7–5.5 V) is back in stock, it is the safer part.
- **PIC:** the SuperCIC key firmware is flashed before soldering, or in place through J3.
