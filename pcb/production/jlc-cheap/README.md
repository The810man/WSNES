# WSNES v0.3: cheap prototype variant

Same board and same gerbers as `../jlc/`. Only the split between JLC and you changes, so the JLC bill is smaller:

- **JLC leaves off five parts:**
  - U1 and U13: out of stock at LCSC and JLC on 2026-11-17
  - SW1, SW2 and U23: optional
- **The green and blue LEDs (D3, D7) become the red JLC basic LED**, KT-0603R `C2286`. They use the same 0603 pads and 1 kΩ resistors, so they run at about 3 mA on 5 V and 1.3 mA on 3.3 V. All three LEDs are then red.
- **Feeder fees drop from 21 extended part types to 15**, which is about 45 USD instead of 63 USD at 3 USD per type.

Generated by `../../tools/export_cheap.py` (run `python3 tools/export_cheap.py .` from `pcb/` after `tools/export_bom.py`). Prices and stock were checked on 2026-11-17.

## Ordering at JLC

1. Upload `../jlc/WSNES_Proto_v0.3_gerbers.zip` and set:
   - 4 layers, 1.2 mm, ENIG
   - gold fingers with bevel
   - smallest via 0.15 mm (one GND via at U12 pin 8 needs it)
   - PCB quantity 5, which is JLC's minimum
2. Turn on **PCB Assembly**:
   - Top side
   - **PCBA quantity 2.** You do not have to assemble all 5 boards: JLC assembles 2 and ships the other 3 bare.
   - Pick Economic if JLC offers it for this board.
3. Upload `WSNES_BOM_JLC_cheap.csv` and `WSNES_CPL_JLC_cheap.csv`. On the parts page, check that every line is matched and in stock.
4. In the placement preview, check rotations. SOT-23, SOT-23-5 and SOT-23-6 parts often need 180°.
5. The last page shows the price. You don't have to order to see it.

To find out what each PCB option costs, toggle it on the quote page. ENIG plus gold fingers is the expensive part. HASL edge fingers work for a bench prototype but wear faster in the slot.

## Notes

- **ESP variant:** any ESP32-S3-WROOM-1 (N4, N8, N16, with or without PSRAM) fits the footprint, because the board doesn't use GPIO35–37. Solder the castellated edge pads. The centre GND pad is optional.
- **Flashing without SW1/SW2:** plug USB-C in and esptool resets the S3 into download mode over native USB (USB-Serial/JTAG). If firmware ever takes over GPIO19/20 and that stops working, bridge the SW1 pads (GPIO0 to GND) while you plug in, or fit the button.
- **U16 (CY62128ELL-45SXI)** is rated 4.5–5.5 V. It sits on VSAVE, one BAT54C drop (≈ 0.35 V) below +5V, so it is only inside its rating while the console delivers about 4.9 V or more. Data retention from the CR2032 is fine (from 2.0 V). When the AS6C1008-55SIN (2.7–5.5 V) is back in stock, it is the safer part.
- **PIC:** flash the SuperCIC key firmware before soldering, or fit J3 and flash it in place.
